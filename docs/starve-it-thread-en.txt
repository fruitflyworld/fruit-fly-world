POST 2 / 8 — THE NEURONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The escape reflex isn't invented. It's the real fruit-fly connectome.

Two visual neurons feed the Giant Fiber — the command cell for the escape jump: LC4 (angular velocity, 2,442 synapses) and LPLC2 (looming, 1,366). Together ~99.6% of its visual input.

In-game it's a leaky integrate-and-fire circuit. The control experiment: real wiring escapes 100% of telegraphed lunges. Shuffled wiring: 68%.

Same seed. Same runs. One number apart.

POST 3 / 8 — THE BRAIN SLOT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Above the brainstem, everything is a SLOT.

A brain is anything that fulfills one contract: given four signals (food, threat, light, novelty) + energy + clock, return one behavior and a confidence.

Four brains ship:
- MANUAL — your hands
- GENES — the wild-type autopilot
- CIRCUIT — a 24-neuron spiking network
- JUDGMENT — a typed judgment model

Same dish. Same rules. Same receipts.

POST 4 / 8 — THE JUDGMENT LAYER (SYSTEM ONE)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The newest brain speaks System One — typed judgment models, not chat.

You ask for a CHOICE among behaviors and a danger SCORE; you get typed answers with confidence.

Why it fits: decisions are parseable, confidence is a first-class number on the HUD, and small judgment models are fast and stable (teams report 92–913× lower variance, ~$0.00035/call — their numbers, not ours).

Pinned versions only. Free offline brain by default. No lock-in.

POST 5 / 8 — THE SEALED LOG
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Every decision from every brain — signals in, distribution out, behavior, confidence, danger read — is sealed with a content hash and downloadable at generation's end (flyline-log/1).

Not "the model said." Instead: here are the 52 decisions, hashed, with the outcome.

The brain chooses; the brainstem jumps. High-level decisions are pluggable. The reflex that keeps you alive is not.

POST 6 / 8 — THE EXAM ROOM
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The whole world derives from one seed, and the game ships an exam room that proves it.

?bench=1 pauses rendering, runs the world TWICE at a fixed 60 Hz on the same seed with the same brain, and compares every decision hash.

Bit-identical → IDENTICAL. Anything else → DIVERGED, and the page says plainly: that's a bug report, not a score.

Our favorite leak it caught: the escape neuron's membrane potential carrying over between runs.
